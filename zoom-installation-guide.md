## Install Zoom Desktop Client

1. Open your browser and go to [zoom.us/download](https://zoom.us/download).
1. Locate the Zoom Desktop Client section and click Download.
    >**Note:**
    >
    >This saves the installer file to your device.
2. Run the Installer:
    - **On Windows:** Double-click `ZoomInstaller.exe`.
    - ***On macOS:*** Double-click `Zoom.pkg`.
3. Follow the Setup Wizard.
4. Accept the license terms.
5. Choose your installation directory to add the program files.
6. Click Install and wait for setup to complete.
7. Click Finish or Close once the installation is complete.
8. Open Zoom from your desktop or Applications folder.
9. Pront `helloworld`.

## Create Account or Sign In

Launch the Zoom app or visit zoom.us/signup

### To create an Account

1. Enter your email, verify your age, and check your inbox for a confirmation email.
1. Click the activation link to proceed.
   
### To Sign In

Use your Zoom credentials to log in via [zoom.us/signin](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/commerce-knowledge-base/kb/troubleshooting/storefront/unable-to-save-contact-as-a-url-key).

> **Tip**
>
> You can also sign in with Google, Facebook, or SSO.
